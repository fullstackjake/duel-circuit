## Where they fit (recommended)

- **A new "No Limits" table above Nyx's**, opened by Nyx's clear: three seats drawn each night from a pool of
  "peak builds" (the deck's real list from its best month, its own banned cards back, plus 2-4 contraband), and a
  champion seat. The player plays with no list too. Targets: your best deck wins 55% (seats 1-3) and 50% (seat 4).
  Stakes lead with each deck's restored card, the only steady source of 2023-26 contraband.
- **A No Limits Invitational** (16 players, best-of-3 final, 10-20% title odds for a champion).
- **The Alley Brawl** gets four named out-of-towners on their legal 2024 decks (Snake-Eye, Yubel, Tenpai, K9), which
  answers its "too easy for a champion" problem with decks that already have ratings and playbooks.
- **Not** Nyx's seats (her theme and 2019 cap), **not** the Midnight Open (already on target), **not** headliners
  (a headliner is one combo, a meta deck is an engine), and a 2025-26 Library cup only later.

## Is 15 the right number?

**Too many to start; build 10 (11 with Toon), cap at 13.** A No Limits table needs 6-8 regulars plus 2 champions, and a player meets
about 8-12 decks over a few nights. With 15, the last five rarely come up, and they cost the most (no deck file, no
playbook).
- **Wave 1 (7, cheap):** Snake-Eye, Mitsurugi, Ryzeal, Vanquish Soul, Tenpai, Branded Despia, Yubel. All in the game,
  rated, with playbooks; only Lacrima and Noroi need small playbook additions. Ships the table and the Brawl.
- **Wave 2 (4):** Kewl Tune (new deck and playbook; Worlds 2026 makes it the natural champion), Toon (the owner's
  pick: new deck and playbook, an authored seat), Maliss (needs a playbook keyed to the peak build only, so the
  Intermediate-band Maliss boss doesn't change), Light and Darkness Ritual (cheap). Ships the Invitational.
  With Toon that's 11 decks, still under the cap of 13.
- **Reserve:** Dracotail, Yummy (both need playbooks), Elfnote.
- **Cut:** Voiceless Voice and Memento (not in the game, no banned cards of their own, borderline ranks).

## Risks and gates

- **AI play:** no peak build ships until its playbook covers its restored cards (`--playbook-ab`, `--turn1`).
- **Difficulty:** a peak Ryzeal could pass 2,050 Elo (the game's top deck is 1,947). Measure against the player's best
  deck under No Limits rules; aim +100 to +200 Elo over the base; tune by how much contraband goes in, never by
  weakening a shipped deck.
- **Miserable cards:** Djinn Releaser of Rituals and Archnemeses Protos should join `Alley.BossOnly`. Tenpai with Cold
  Wave, Trunade and Raigeki is a real going-second kill; Elfnote can strip the hand to 2 on turn 1.
- **Data gap:** lflists/ has no TCG lists from April 2025 to September 2026; add them before building.
- **Toon's cards are OCG-only in the game today:** the player gets them from the OCG import packs until Magnificent
  Maestros lands in the printings data. `PrintingIndex.FirstYear` counts TCG printings only (0 for these), so a
  generated Toon splash would fail the era check: Toon needs an authored seat. Its playbook needs new logic for Mind
  Scan's name declaration, and must use Grinder Golem only when Comic Cat can Tribute it. It's fragile to losing
  Perfect World (the player's out), so no Cold Wave or floodgates to protect it.
- **The Alley list trims engines** (Tenpai's Chundra and Kaimen, Ryzeal's Ext Ryzeal to 1); No Limits rules avoid that.

#### 1.3 The No Limits table

- **Rules for you:** no list. Three of anything, except the alley's `Never` cards (Elma, Wind-Up Hunter and the other
  loops the bots can't stop) and the `BossOnly` cards (floodgates, Barrier Statues, Magical Scientist). The planned name
  is "No Limits", and the payoff is symmetry: you can finally run what they run. [P] owner decision 1.
- **Rules for them:** CPUs are exempt, as everywhere. Each peak build plays its authored list at skill 1.0. Nothing is
  cut after it ships (owner rule).
- **Seats.** Four a night, like the Den tables.
  - Seats 1-3: drawn (seeded, saved) from the regular pool of peak builds, three different decks a night.
  - Seat 4: the champion's seat. Two champions, used like a table's two headliners: one closes, the other can
    wildcard into seats 1-3 (1/3), encore after both are beaten. Wave 1: Snake-Eye (the deck of the three years) closes;
    Kewl Tune (Worlds 2026) is `ready: no` until wave 2. If measurement shows another deck is stronger in the engine,
    it takes seat 4 instead: fame picks the pair, the measurement confirms it.
- **New code (engineer's call, flagged):** an `AlleyTier` beyond `{Street, Den, Orvel, Nyx}`; a seat kind that draws
  authored decks from a pool (Nyx's seats are fixed today); an `Alley.Events` entry with era cap 2026 (9999); a
  ratings path for the authored decks (`--rate-decks` skips decks/alley/, and brackets refuse unrated decks).
- **Stakes.** A No Limits stake [P] (Economy Designer; above Den's 1,000 held DP). The CPU's binder leads with **its
  own restored card** (headliner-style `stakes:`), so beating the Snake-Eye pilot can win you an Original Sinful Spoils
  - Snake-Eye. That is the only steady source of 2023-26 contraband, which makes the table worth repeating.
- **Rewards.** First clear: a token and a poster [P]. Every first win against a build: its Unlisted page. Repeat
  nights: only the cards won (the alley's rule).

#### 2.1 From the game's structure

| Slot | Decks it needs | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| No Limits table, seats 1-3 | 6 minimum (two nights with no repeat), 8 good | You meet 3 a night. 8 regulars rotate in about 3 nights |
| No Limits table, seat 4 | 2 champions | The headliner pattern: one closes, one wildcards |
| Invitational | 15 CPU seats | 10 peak builds + 5 alley names. You play 4 matches a run, so you meet 4 of the 15 |
| Alley Brawl | 4 pilots | Existing 2024 decks; no new deck files |

- **How many distinct opponents you meet.** Table: 4 on the first clear, about 8-12 over 3-4 nights. Invitational: 4 a
  run, about 8-10 distinct over 3 runs. With 10 builds you see every one within that play. With 15 you'd need about 5
  nights; the last five would rarely come up, and they are the costliest five.
- **Repetition.** Ranks 3, 4, 10 and 12 already appear 3-5 times each in the end game. Adding more of the same family
  isn't more content; it's the same engine with one card changed.
- **Difficulty curve.** The top of the alley is a plateau (Nyx 55%, Orvel 55-60%). Ten builds give enough spread to
  set seats 1-3 at 55% and seat 4 at 50% without padding the pool with weaker decks.

### 4. Owner decisions (recommendation first)

1. **No Limits rules for you:** no list, 3 of anything, minus `Never` and `BossOnly`. *Or the Alley list (one copy),

2. **The number:** 10 in two waves, 3 reserves, cap 13; cut Voiceless Voice and Memento. *Or all 15.*
3. **The Brawl fix:** four named out-of-towners on their 2024 decks; add a Basement splash only if `--bracket-sim`
   still shows over 45%. By Elo alone, as built gives a champion about 55%, splashed about 45-50%; measure it. *Or
   judge the Brawl at the strong-player band (35-56% today).*
4. **The Maliss playbook:** keyed to the peak build only. *Or on `maliss` too, re-measuring the Link Age Open and the
   Ryzeal cup.*
5. **Nyx's seats 1-3:** fixed with era-true material, not meta builds. *Or meta builds at Nyx (breaks her theme).*
6. **A 2025-26 Library cup:** later, shelved outside the Every Era count. *Or skip it.*

### 5. Notes for the deck designer (contraband by placement)

- **No Limits peak builds:**
  - first, the deck's own Forbidden and Limited cards back at 3: that is its identity;
  - then 2-4 modern (2020-2026) ever-Forbidden cards that fit its engine or its weak matchups, not raw power;
  - at most one generic "power" card (Pot of Greed, Harpie's Feather Duster, Raigeki and the like) per deck, so the
    builds don't converge;
  - no `Never` or `BossOnly` cards; Fiendsmith in at most 3 builds; no two builds sharing more than 2 contraband cards.
- **Seat 4 champions** (Snake-Eye, Kewl Tune): the fullest builds, still inside the +250 Elo cap.
- **Alley Brawl pilots:** no contraband (their as-built decks). If a splash is needed, Basement-era (2014) cards only.
- **Invitational:** the peak builds as authored. Nothing new.
- **Every build:** a `#banlist` naming its peak month's list, a `#recipe` naming the base so its playbook plays it,
  and a `stakes:` line led by its restored card.


## Appendix B: Deck designer (contraband)

Date: 2026-11-02. Read-only design pass. I edited no repo files.

Inputs: `docs/dev/META-RESEARCH-2026-11-02.md`, `scratchpad/meta/inventory.md`, the 14 base decks in `decks/history/`, `vendor/BabelCDB/*.cdb`, `lflists/history.lflist.conf` plus `vendor/LFLists/0TCG.lflist.conf` and `Traditional.lflist.conf`, and `src/Ygo.Engine/Campaign/Alley.cs`.

### 0. How every card here was checked

- **Ever-Forbidden:** I rebuilt `Alley.Contraband` in a script. It takes every code at 0 in any list whose name has "TCG", from both list files. That gives 264 cards. Minus the 33 `Excluded` cards, that is 231, which matches the inventory.
- **Every pick below is in that pool.** The "Forbidden" column gives the first and last TCG list with the card at 0, as the repo has them.
- **In the DB and scripted:** every pool card has a row in the cdb and a `vendor/CardScripts/official/c<code>.lua`. I checked all 264.
- **"Today" column** is the card's limit on `0TCG` (2026.10 TCG):
  - **F**: Forbidden today, so 1 copy in the Alley (Traditional).
  - **L**: Limited today.
  - **3**: legal at 3 today. It was Forbidden once, so it is in the pool, but it does not feel like a banned card any more.
- **E** = Extra Deck card. **A** = needs an authored deck, because it needs engine cards the base doesn't run, or more than one Extra Deck card.
- **Never / BossOnly** are the `Alley.Never` / `Alley.BossOnly` lists. No pick below is a Never card. The only BossOnly cards are marked as such, under Labrynth.

**Repo gap:** `lflists/` has no TCG lists between April 2025 and September 2026. So White Binder shows as Forbidden from Sep 2026, not Feb 2026, and Noroi from Sep 2026, not May 2026. Any card that Konami Forbade and then freed inside that gap is missing from the pool. I found no deck in the 15 that loses a key card to this gap.

### 1. Findings that shape the whole design

1. **Most 2026 bases already carry their own banned cards.** These lists are from before the 19 Oct list, so they were legal when played. Traditional keeps them at 1. So "its own banned card comes back" happens without any change for:

   | Base | Banned cards it already runs |
   |---|---|
   | snake-eye | Original Sinful Spoils, Crossout, TTT, Called, Apollousa |
   | maliss | White Binder, Splash Mage, Firewall Dragon |
   | mitsurugi | Habakiri, Magicians' Souls, Dragoon, Ilia Silvia, Zenna's, Charmer Quartet, Cross-Sheep |
   | yummy | Cupsy★Yummy Way, Magicians' Souls, Dragoon, Witch, Linkuriboh |
   | branded-despia | Albion, Norden, Called, Super Poly |
   | light-and-darkness | the same six as Mitsurugi, plus Firewall Dragon |
   | elfnote | Tinia, Crystal Wing, F.A. Dawn Dragster |
   | ryzeal (extra) | Abyss Dweller, Exciton Knight, King of the Feral Imps, Dugares |
   | yubel (extra) | Aerial Eater, Varudras, D/D/D Wave High King Caesar, Moon of the Closed Heaven |

   The hooks that really need adding are:
   - Fiendsmith's Lacrima, for Yubel and Snake-Eye.
   - K9-04 Noroi, for Vanquish Soul.
   - Kewl Tune Rotary, for Kewl Tune, which has no deck yet.
   - Branded Expulsion, for Branded.

   So the picks below aim at a second idea per deck.
2. **The Alley list trims some bases before any splash.** `Loaners.Adjust` cuts each base to the Alley list. That frees slots, but in some decks it costs engine copies:

   | Base | Cards cut to the Alley limit |
   |---|---|
   | tenpai-dragon | Chundra 3→1, Sangen Kaimen 3→1, Dimension Shifter 3→1 (engine hit) |
   | ryzeal | Ext Ryzeal 3→1 (engine hit), Droll 3→1, Bonfire 3→1, TTT 2→1 |

   | maliss | White Rabbit 2→1, Dormouse 2→1 |
   | snake-eye | Bonfire, Crossout 3→1; TTT 2→1 |
   | mitsurugi, light-and-darkness | Griffoh 3→1, Droll 2→1 |
   | branded-despia | Griffoh 3→1, Droll 2→1, High Spirits 2→1 |
   | yubel | Droll 3→1, Phantom of Yubel 2→1 |
   | sky-striker | Engage 3→2 |
   | yummy, elfnote | Droll 2→1 |
   | dracotail | none |

   An authored deck can't go above the Alley list either. Where the cuts below say "freed by the list", the splash fills slots the list already emptied.
3. **No current event can take these decks.** Era caps are 2006 / 2014 / 2019, and `FromEra` needs 90% of a deck's cards out by the cap. The Black Market's `MaxExtra` is 0.
   - These belong to a new top table, such as the planned "No Limits" table with an era cap of 2026.
   - Or they can be authored seats (`decks/alley/*.ydk` with `#recipe <base>`).
   - The curated `alley.txt` is Main Deck only. The builder allows one Extra Deck card, and only through a base's `for:` list. Every **E** pick needs that `for:` line or an authored deck.
4. **These bases are already the strongest decks in the game:** Ryzeal 1867, Branded 1830, LADR 1768, Mitsurugi 1742. The measured splash gain is about +50 Elo, so a contraband modern table sits far above the Black Market's 1585-1693 window. Three bases have no playbook and rate low: Maliss 1392, Yummy 1198, Dracotail 1549. A playbook matters more than contraband for them. The repo's own evidence is the Gravekeeper's deck: the player's win rate against it fell from 82.5% to 56.7% once it played on a playbook.
5. **Generic layer, kept out of the tables:**
   - Maxx "C" (F) fits every hand-trap shell: Snake-Eye, Kewl Tune, Yummy, Voiceless Voice, Ryzeal.
   - Pot of Greed and Graceful Charity (F) are right only where Spells are fuel (Sky Striker), or where draw is the plan.
   - Called by the Grave, Crossout Designator and Triple Tactics Talent (F since Oct 2026) are already in the real 2024-2026 lists. They are not reimaginings.

#### 6.2 The reimagined deck

**Hook:** "Steal it, eat it, draw it." Comic Cat already Tributes the opponent's monster. The banned cards that hand monsters across the table (Grinder Golem, Change of Heart) or pay out when Tributed (Witch, Sangan, Crush Card Virus) turn every Tribute into a card. Mind Scan's open hand makes the banned hand-strip Spells fully informed.

| Card | Today | Forbidden | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Grinder Golem | F | Oct 2019 - now | It goes to the opponent's field, so Comic Cat can Tribute it from there and summon a Toon from the Deck. You keep two DARK Fiend tokens, which are the 2 Tributes Blue-Eyes Toon Dragon needs, or Link fodder. This is the hook. |
| Witch of the Black Forest | 3 | Apr 2005 - Jun 2017 | Comic Cat's ideal Tribute. It searches DEF 1500 or less: Funny Dark Rabbit (1500), Comic Cat (0), Faceless Mage (0), Toon Summoned Skull (1200). It covers for the Alley list cutting Table of Contents and Bookmark to 1. |
| Sangan | 3 | Mar 2013 - Aug 2016 | The ATK 1500-or-less twin: Rabbit (1100), Comic Cat, Faceless Mage, Cartoon Shade. |
| Change of Heart | 3 | Apr 2005 - Jul 2021 | Takes a monster for the turn: one of Blue-Eyes Toon Dragon's 2 Tributes, Link material for Perfectron, or a blocker gone before the direct attacks. |
| Crush Card Virus | 3 | Mar 2010 - Oct 2014 | Tributes a DARK monster with 1000 ATK or less: Comic Cat, Faceless Mage and Cartoon Shade (0 ATK) qualify, so it passes `dark-low-atk:3`. Wipes their 1500+ monsters before Toons swing. |
| Harpie's Feather Duster | L | Apr 2005 - Jun 2020 | Clears only *their* Spells and Traps before the direct attacks. Heavy Storm and Giant Trunade are left out on purpose: they would also hit (or bounce) Toon World the Perfect World, which every Toon needs. |
| Confiscation | F | Apr 2005 - now | Mind Scan keeps their hand revealed, so the discard is chosen with full information: take the hand trap before Comic Cat and Rabbit go. |

None of these is Never or BossOnly. Delinquent Duo (F) is an alternative to Confiscation; the second discard is the opponent's choice, so it is worse here.

- **Cuts:**
  - The Alley list trims Toon Table of Contents 3→1 and Toon Bookmark 3→1. These are TCG Limited on 19 Oct 2026, and the real lists run 3 of each. That frees 4 slots but costs Perfect World searches. Witch and Sangan take 2 of those slots to give the searching back.
  - The rest come from the hand-trap block of the winning list: about 3 of Ash / Ghost Ogre / Droll class.
  - Keep 3 Perfect World (3 in the Alley), 3 Funny Dark Rabbit, 2-3 Comic Cat, Evil Box, Faceless Mage, Mind Scan and Toon Terror.
  - Extra Deck: no contraband pick. Perfectron is already a 1-of (Limited).
- **Feasibility:**
  - **No deck file and no playbook.** The research doc names the Japan Championship 2026 list (Kobayashi Ryohei, ygoprodeck); it would need to be fetched and mapped with `tools/build_deck.py`.
  - All engine cards are in the DB and scripted.
  - It needs a profile, a portrait, a rating (`--rate-decks`), and an authored `decks/alley/*.ydk` with `#recipe toon` once a playbook exists.
  - A Ryzeal Toon build (two Funny Dark Rabbit make Rank 4 Ryzeal Duo Drive) could use the existing ryzeal Extra Deck, but RyzealPlaybook knows no Toon card.
- **What the playbook must know:**
  1. **Perfect World first**, then use its search up to 3 times: Rabbit, Comic Cat, Evil Box, Table of Contents, Bookmark. Funny Dark Rabbit places Perfect World if it is the starter.
  2. **Rabbit's extra Normal Summon** goes on a Toon World monster, not on a hand trap.
  3. **Comic Cat** is a Main Phase Quick Effect. Tribute the opponent's best monster, on either player's turn. Pull Toon Black Luster Soldier first: it is the only puller that can attack the turn it arrives, and it banishes a card. Then Evil Box or Blue-Eyes Toon Dragon.
  4. **Evil Box**: Special Summon it with Toon World up and set Toon Terror. Its quick effect bottoms the key card in the opponent's GY.
  5. **Faceless Mage**: discard it to place Mind Scan, or later to revive a Toon World monster.
  6. **Mind Scan's name declaration**: name the most dangerous card in the opponent's *revealed* hand. That is new logic for the bot.
  7. **Perfect World's banish dodge** in response to removal. Toon Terror against Spell/Trap or effect removal.
  8. **Blue-Eyes Toon Ultimate Dragon** contact Fusion by shuffling materials back; then all Toons attack directly. Toon attacks cost 500 LP each.
  9. **Grinder Golem only on a turn when Comic Cat can Tribute it**, or Blue-Eyes Toon Dragon is in hand. Never just to make tokens, or it hands the player a 3000 beater.
- **Power flags:**
  - **OTK risk: real, not first-turn.** There is no battle on turn 1, so no FTK; the research doc could not explain the "Toon FTK" label, and I see no first-turn kill in these cards either. From turn 2 on, a realistic line (Harpie's Feather Duster, then Comic Cat or Crush Card Virus to clear blockers) is:
    - Toon Black Luster Soldier, 3000 direct, pulled by Comic Cat and able to attack that turn;
    - plus a Toon Summoned Skull or Blue-Eyes Toon Dragon held from the turn before;
    - plus Blue-Eyes Toon Ultimate Dragon, which makes every Toon attack directly;
    - or Perfectron's Link Summon wipe (5000 ATK).

    That is lethal going second, and the deck's job. Toon Summoned Skull and Blue-Eyes Toon Dragon "cannot attack the turn" they are Special Summoned, which keeps the burst to one Toon Black Luster Soldier plus whatever survived a turn.
  - **Hand control:** Mind Scan plus Confiscation means the player plays face-up and loses their best card on turn 1. Frustrating, but outable. Keep one hand-strip card at most.
  - **Bot misplay risk:** Grinder Golem (gives a 3000 ATK monster away) and Change of Heart timing. Both need playbook rules, or leave them out of a generated splash.
  - **Fragility:** everything dies if Perfect World goes. Player Field Spell removal is the intended out, and Bookmark covers it once. Don't add anything that protects the Field Spell further: no Cold Wave, no BossOnly floodgates.
