# Proof pass (POST-GAME-PLAN step 11), 2026-11-01

Step 11 asks for an earned/spent ledger, post-game pilots (minimal, collector, gambler), an arbitrage bot over every
loop, and a full QA run from a champion save, all checked against the plan's targets
([POST-GAME-PLAN](POST-GAME-PLAN-2026-10-31.md), "## Targets").

**Who did what:**
- **Senior Developer:** the ledger, the pilots, the arbitrage bot, and the champion-save writer.
- **Unity Architect:** the QA stand-in in the post-game.
- **Reality Checker:** an audit of the guarantees against the tests.
- **Economy Designer:** an independent read of the economy results.
- **Test Results Analyzer:** an independent read of the post-game QA run.

## Verdict

**The economy holds.** One rule broke it (level-up DP); it was fixed the same day, and every target the tools can measure
now holds:
- cup farming pays 150 DP a duel;
- Every Era repeats average 209 DP a duel, with first wins and the prize included;
- a Locals title run nets −100 DP, paid back in Prize Packs;
- no buy-and-sell loop makes DP.

The rest of this section is the analysis from before the fix.

**Before the fix: most of the economy held, and one rule broke it.**
- **Holding:**
  - No buy-and-sell loop makes DP.
  - Spending targets are met by every persona that buys anything.
  - The post-game plays through without a single flow break.
- **Breaking:** level-up DP. Every duel earns EXP, and levels keep paying DP with no ceiling until level 99, so post-game
  duels pay more than the 300 DP-a-duel cap. A Locals title run nets DP before its prize. The fix is recommended below,
  and it's the owner's call.

## The level-up DP fix (applied 2026-11-01, the owner's go-ahead)

Two agents recommend the same change: **duels against an opponent you've already beaten (Library cup repeats, Every Era
repeats) and Locals duels still earn EXP, but the level-ups they reach pay Card Tokens and no DP.**
- **Cup farming:** 150 DP a duel. Every Era repeats: about 150.
- **Locals:** placement minus entry, −416 an entry at 30% title odds and −349 at 45%, for 1-2 Prize Packs (about 1,600
  DP of cards each, which can't be sold back). A clean sink that pays in cards.
- **Spending shares:** a gambler goes from 38.7% to about 40%; minimal and collector barely move.
- **What it keeps:** first wins and retries of matches not yet won keep paying level DP, so the weakest players aren't
  hurt.
- **Saves:** a pays-DP flag at the call sites, with no new save field and no clawback.
- **As built:** `Leveling.AddExp(profile, exp, payDp)`. Library cup repeats and Every Era repeats pass `payDp: false`
  when the match was already won; Locals always do. Those levels pay their usual tokens, at least 1. A `--campaign-test`
  check covers it, and `--arbitrage` passes the cup-farming, Every Era and Locals lines.
- **On screen:** "Level 53! +1 Card Token" (tokens sell for nothing).
- **Rejected alternatives:**
  - level DP from first wins only: hurts losses and retries;
  - flattening levels past 40: lands near a normal Gauntlet clear and hits Master grinders;
  - an entry fee: fixes Locals only;
  - accepting it: fails the 300 cap.

## The post-game QA run (from the champion save)

60 duels in 58 minutes, 0 issues; a Test Results Analyzer read the log and screenshots.
- **Covered:** 5 cups cleared (8.2 duels each on average), 3 stamps, 5 era mats and 4 engravings, 5 Locals entries (13
  duels, 1 title, 4 Prize Packs).
- **The ledger reconciles exactly:** 41,325 DP in, 62,010 out.
- **What works:**
  - the flow (cup, mat, engraving, entry, bracket, finish, packs);
  - placement pay (900 / 650 / 400 / 325 for a loaner finalist);
  - plates (gold at stamped cups, silver at HAT, none at TeleDAD);
  - half pay with loaners;
  - Stuck? after 3 losses, and Make it yours.
- **Fixed from it:**
  - **The Prize Pack reveal never showed the slab.** A sealed Grade 9/10 read "Raw · Mint". It now shows in its case:
    "Sealed slab · 10 Gem Mint".
  - **The Every Era final's round screen could never offer Make it yours** (found while building the QA stand-in). A
    player with no legal deck can't start the round to lose it three times. With no legal deck, it now opens Stuck?
    with the offer at once.
  - **Smaller:**
    - Library results now say "CUP PROGRESS";
    - Engrave says "Win here to engrave" when your deck hasn't won at the cup;
    - the Locals entry says the other duelists bring their decks as built.
- **Noted (QA tool, not the game):**
  - The stand-in keeps an unmeasured legal deck over better advice.
  - It played an unmeasured loaner in a final.
  - Two of its purchase lines name the wrong deck.
  - Goat Cup match 1 went 1 for 6 against a measured 75%. It's deterministic: replay its seeds in the harness before
    reading anything into it.

## The main-campaign sim

- **A bot bug skewed it.** Its selection-pack loop opened packs until DP ran out, because a pack's card count never
  shrinks. That made 4th-copy refunds 35-40% of earnings and spending about 100%. Fixed: the bot now stops when the
  target deck is complete.
- **The fixed run** (`build/proof-economy/campaign-sim-fixed.log`, seed 1):
  - It cleared 14 of 22 circuits in 138 duels, then **stalled at Expert Series: Rituals and Fire match 4** (Kenta
    Mizorogi's Tenpai Dragon): 3 wins in 38, at the casual pilot's skill of 0.6.
  - So it never wrote the normal-wallet champion save.
  - It still spends about 100% of earnings each band (48 DP left), because it buys boosters whenever it has no deck to
    aim for. That's the bot's spending policy, not the game's.
  - Card unlocks came to 0.7-2.4% of each band's collection growth (cap 15%).
- **What it did confirm is real:** selection packs dominate spending at every band at a flat 200 DP. That's change 1 in
  [PACKS-REVIEW-2026-11-01.md](PACKS-REVIEW-2026-11-01.md) section 3.

## Tools now available (harness: `build/bin/check/Ygo.Harness.dll`)

- `--campaign-sim [--seed N] [--save <mods dir>]`: the main campaign with the ledger per band; `--save` writes a champion
  save the app loads with `-mods`.
- `--postgame-sim [--load <mods dir>] [--persona frugal|minimal|collector|gambler|all] [--cups 10]`
- `--arbitrage [--load <mods dir>]`: every loop; fails on any DP-positive one.
- **The ledger:** `Profile.Earn` / `Profile.Spend` for every DP change, saved as `earned`/`spent` lines.
  - Sources: `circuit`, `tag`, `guest`, `cup`, `final`, with `.prize` and `.loss`; `locals`, `levelup`, `refund.*`,
    `sell`, `passrefund`.
  - Sinks: `packs`, `packs.selection`, `recipe.pack`, `recipe.outright`, `makeityours`, `structure`, `tokens`,
    `grading`, `used`, `order`, `mats`, `engrave`, `brackets`.
- **The app:** `-mods <champion save> -qaplay <out> -qacontinue -qagames N -qatools` plays the post-game: cups,
  cosmetics, Locals, Prize Packs and the final.
