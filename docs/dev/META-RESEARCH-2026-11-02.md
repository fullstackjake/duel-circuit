# TCG Meta Research: the 15 strongest decks, Nov 2023 to Nov 2026

Date: 2026-11-02. Scope: real-life Yu-Gi-Oh! TCG, Advanced Format. No game design suggestions.

## 0. Bottom line

- Snake-Eye (with its Fire King hybrid) is the clear number one of the three years. It was the top deck for nearly all of 2024.
- Maliss, Mitsurugi and Ryzeal came next. They ran the meta from December 2024 to September 2025.
- In 2026, Kewl Tune had the biggest single-format peak since Ryzeal. It also won Worlds 2026.
- Toon is an OCG deck, not yet a TCG one. Its new support is not TCG-legal until Magnificent Maestros (13 Nov 2026), so it is outside the ranked 15. In the OCG it was a top-two deck from June to September 2026. If OCG results counted, it would sit around #10-12. See section 8b.
- Ranks 1-9 are well supported. Ranks 10-13 are fairly solid. Ranks 14-15 are a judgement call among about six close decks (see section 3).
- The data is thick for 2024 to mid-2026. It is thin for November-December 2023 and for September-November 2026.

## 1. Method

### Research question

Which TCG archetypes were most successful and most played at major events between 1 Nov 2023 and 2 Nov 2026? Success is measured by premier top-cut share, major wins, time spent at tier 1/2, and banlist impact.

### Search strategy

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Primary data | YGOPRODeck tournament database, queried through the site's own JSON endpoints (the same ones its Top Archetypes page uses). TCG format only. |
| Event tiers used | ygoprodeck "Tier 3 (Premier)": YCS, WCQs, National and State/Area Championships, WCQ playoffs. Also "Tier 2+" (adds Regionals and similar) as a cross-check. Worlds is ygoprodeck "Tier 4" and was read separately. |
| Cross-checks | Konami's official event coverage (yugiohblog.konami.com) for NAWCQ 2024, YCS Orlando 2025 and TEAM YCS Las Vegas 2026. Konami press pages for Worlds. |
| Banlists | Konami's official list pages (Sept 21 and Oct 19 2026), YuGiOh Card Guide archived list pages (Jan 2024 to Feb 2026), YGOrganization (Sept 2025, May 2026), YuGiOh Meta (list date history). |
| Date range | 1 Nov 2023 to 2 Nov 2026, split into the 12 banlist periods that overlap it. |
| Inclusion | Any archetype with premier top-cut entries in the window. |
| Exclusion | OCG, Master Duel, Genesys and Rush data were excluded from scoring. Worlds uses its own special list, so it is reported as a result but not as a format. |
| Results | 225 Tier-3 TCG events in the window. 5,011 premier top-cut entries. 242 premier event wins. Plus 3 TCG World Championships. Tier-2+ adds about 18,000 top-cut entries. |

### Scoring

Four signals, in this order of weight:

1. **Time-weighted top-cut share (TW).** For each banlist period, the deck's share of premier top-cut entries, weighted by how many days that period lasted in the window. Summed over 36 months. This rewards both dominance and staying power. It also stops spring Nationals season (which has many more events) from swamping the count.
2. **Major wins.** YCS, WCQ main events and Worlds count most. Nationals count less.
3. **Formats at tier 1 or tier 2.** Tier 1 here means 10% or more of premier top cuts in that period. Tier 2 means 4-10%.
4. **Banlist impact.** Cards Forbidden, Limited or Semi-Limited that were core to the deck.

The ranking is a judgement over these four. It is not a single formula. Where two decks are close, the text says so.

### Known weaknesses of the data

- ygoprodeck classifies each deck by its primary archetype ("arch_1"). Hybrids are split by that label. My merge rules (section 4) change a few numbers.
- The database is volunteer-entered. Coverage of Nov-Dec 2023 premier events is thin (96 entries). Some winners have no deck recorded (for example YCS Quito 2025 and the team YCS events).
- ygoprodeck counts 16-player WCQ playoff events as Tier 3. These add a few "wins". I exclude them from the "notable results" lists below.
- Top-cut share measures success, not field share. Konami's full-event play rates exist for only a few events (NAWCQ 2024 is used as a check).
- Deck "game plan" lines are my own one-sentence summaries. They are based on the winning lists I read and the coverage cited. They are not individually sourced.

### 1. Snake-Eye (Fire King Snake-Eye, Diabellstar Snake-Eye, Fiendsmith Snake-Eye, Kashtira Snake-Eye)

- **Peak:** Feb 2024 to Dec 2024 (P2-P4). First premier tops in the data: Fire King builds from Dec 2023, Snake-Eye from 17 Feb 2024.
- **Share:** 60.6% of premier top cuts in P2 (Snake-Eye 27.4% plus Fire King-labelled 33.2%; only 226 entries). 40.3% in P3. 24.0% in P4.
- **Key cards:** Snake-Eye Ash, Original Sinful Spoils - Snake-Eye, Diabellstar the Black Witch. Fire King builds add Sacred Fire King Garunix and Promethean Princess. From July 2024, the Fiendsmith engine (Fiendsmith Engraver).
- **Game plan:** Going-first combo. It mills and revives FIRE monsters to build a multi-interruption board, and it still has follow-up if the board is broken.
- **Notable results:** YCS Sydney (9 Mar 2024), YCS Medellin (30 Mar 2024), YCS Guadalajara (13 Apr 2024), YCS Raleigh (20 Apr 2024), YCS Rio de Janeiro (4 May 2024), YCS Indianapolis (1 Jun 2024), Central America WCQ (21 Jun 2024), Oceanic WCQ (28 Jun 2024), North America WCQ (19 Jul 2024, 2,635 players), YCS Sacramento (17 Aug 2024). Fire King-labelled builds won YCS Niagara Falls (12 Oct 2024) and YCS Guayaquil (2 Nov 2024). Worlds 2024: runner-up plus most of the top 32 (14 of 28 decks were Snake-Eye; ygoprodeck WCS 2024 page).
- **Konami check:** At NAWCQ 2024, Fiendsmith Snake-Eye was 669 of 2,635 decks (about 25%) and 29 of the Top 64.
- **Banlist:** Snake-Eye Ash and Snake-Eyes Poplar went to Limited (2 Sep 2024). Original Sinful Spoils - Snake-Eye was Forbidden (Dec 2024 list). Ash and Poplar went back to Semi-Limited (7 Apr 2025), then off the list (15 Sep 2025). Fiendsmith's Lacrima, which Snake-Eye and Yubel shared, was Forbidden on 2 Sep 2024. Original Sinful Spoils - Snake-Eye is still Forbidden on the 19 Oct 2026 list.

### 2. Maliss (Bystial Maliss, @Ignister Maliss, Fiendsmith Maliss)

- **Peak:** Dec 2024 to Feb 2026 (P5-P9). Still topping in mid-2026.
- **Share:** 23.3% (P5), 26.9% (P6), 13.3% (P8). Tier 2 in P7 and P9.
- **Key cards:** Maliss <P> Dormouse, Maliss <P> White Rabbit, Maliss in Underground. Later lists pair it with Backup @Ignister.
- **Game plan:** Going-first Cyberse Link combo. It banishes its own monsters for value and uses Maliss Traps to interrupt.
- **Notable results:** France Nationals (31 May 2025, 2,480 players), South America WCQ (7 Jun 2025), Central America WCQ (5 Jul 2025), LATAM Remote Duel YCS (31 Jan 2026). Runner-up and Top 4 at NAWCQ 2025. 18 of the NAWCQ 2025 Top 64 (tied first with Mitsurugi). 17 Bystial Maliss in the YCS Orlando 2025 Top 64 (Konami).
- **Banlist:** Dormouse, White Rabbit and Maliss in Underground to Semi-Limited (7 Apr 2025). Dormouse to Limited (15 Sep 2025). Maliss <Q> White Binder Forbidden (2 Feb 2026). White Rabbit to Limited (May 2026 list).

### 3. Mitsurugi (Ryzeal Mitsurugi, Fiendsmith Mitsurugi, Azamina Mitsurugi)

- **Peak:** Apr 2025 to Oct 2025 (P6-P7). Still a tier-2 piece into 2026.
- **Share:** 33.0% of premier top cuts in P6, the highest of that period. 17.8% in P7.
- **Key cards:** Mitsurugi Prayers, Ame no Habakiri no Mitsurugi, Mitsurugi Ritual. The 2025 lists also run the Ryzeal engine (Ice Ryzeal, Ryzeal Detonator).
- **Game plan:** Ritual Reptile deck. It Tributes monsters to trigger effects and uses on-field negation and removal from either turn.
- **Notable results:** LATAM Remote Duel YCS (8 Feb 2025), YCS Providence (3 May 2025, 1,572 players), UK Nationals (14 Jun 2025). Runner-up at YCS Lille (Sep 2025). Runner-up at YCS Orlando (Feb 2025), per a Konami winner post seen only as a search snippet. 18 of the NAWCQ 2025 Top 64. 18 of the YCS Lille 2025 Top 64.
- **Banlist:** Ame no Habakiri no Mitsurugi to Limited (2 Feb 2026), then Forbidden (19 Oct 2026). Note that Mitsurugi had no card hit during its 2025 peak.

### 4. Ryzeal (Fiendsmith Ryzeal, Heraldic Ryzeal)

- **Peak:** Dec 2024 to Apr 2025 (P5).
- **Share:** 47.7% of premier top cuts in P5. This is the largest single-period share of any deck in the window.
- **Key cards:** Ice Ryzeal, Sword Ryzeal, Ryzeal Detonator.
- **Game plan:** Going-first Rank 4 Xyz combo. It ends on Ryzeal Detonator plus generic Rank 4 interruption, often with the Fiendsmith engine.
- **Notable results:** YCS Anaheim (7 Dec 2024), YCS Orlando (1 Feb 2025, 2,223-2,274 players), YCS Sydney (1 Mar 2025), Oceanic WCQ (5 Jul 2025). Konami's YCS Orlando Top 64 had 24 Fiendsmith Ryzeal, 7 Mitsurugi Ryzeal and 4 pure Ryzeal (35 Ryzeal decks of 64).
- **Banlist:** Ryzeal Detonator to Limited, and Ext Ryzeal, Ice Ryzeal and Sword Ryzeal to Semi-Limited (7 Apr 2025). Ext Ryzeal to Limited (15 Sep 2025).
- **Note:** Much of Ryzeal's 2025 success is counted under Mitsurugi, because ygoprodeck files "Ryzeal Mitsurugi" there.

### 5. Kewl Tune

- **Peak:** Mar 2026 to Sep 2026 (P9-P10). First premier top in the data: 14 Feb 2026.
- **Share:** 7.0% (P9), then 31.1% (P10).
- **Key cards:** Kewl Tune Cue, Kewl Tune Rotary, Kewl Tune Reco and Kewl Tune Mix. Lists also run Fydraulis Harmonia and Synchro Overtake.
- **Game plan:** Synchro combo deck that Synchro Summons on both players' turns. The winning lists carry 15+ hand traps and board breakers, so it plays both first and second.
- **Notable results:** France Nationals (23 May 2026, 2,954 players), Central America WCQ (6 Jun 2026), UK Nationals (13 Jun 2026), Italy Nationals (20 Jun 2026), South America WCQ (20 Jun 2026), European WCQ (11 Jul 2026, 2,268 players), World Championship 2026 (Mario De Micco, 30 Aug 2026; 9 of 30 Worlds decks and 3 of the Top 8 were Kewl Tune), LATAM Remote Duel YCS (19 Sep 2026). At NAWCQ 2026, 19 of the Top 32 were Kewl Tune, but Sky Striker won.
- **Banlist:** Kewl Tune Rotary Forbidden (21 Sep 2026). Synchro Overtake went to Limited in May 2026. Kewl Tune lists play it, but it is a generic card, so I do not count it as a Kewl Tune hit.

### 6. Yummy (Mitsurugi Yummy, Fiendsmith Yummy, Radiant Typhoon Tri-Brigade Yummy)

- **Peak:** Aug 2025 to May 2026 (P7-P9).
- **Share (Yummy plus Mitsurugi Yummy):** 35.0% (P7, only 157 entries), 27.7% (P8), 15.0% (P9).
- **Key cards:** Lollipo☆Yummy, Marshmao☆Yummy, Cupsy★Yummy Way, Yummy★Snatchy.
- **Game plan:** Synchro/Link Beast combo with a strong grind game. Most top lists splice it with Mitsurugi or the Fiendsmith engine.
- **Notable results:** YCS Vancouver (16 Aug 2025), YCS Lima (27 Sep 2025), YCS Merida (29 Nov 2025), 300th YCS Richmond (14 Feb 2026, 2,284 players). Worlds 2025: Top 4 and Top 8. Konami's TEAM YCS Las Vegas 2026 Top 8 (24 decks across 8 teams) had 6 Fiendsmith Yummy, the most of any deck.
- **Banlist:** Yummy★Snatchy to Limited (2 Feb 2026). Cupsy☆Yummy and Cupsy★Yummy Way to Limited (May 2026). Cupsy★Yummy Way Forbidden (19 Oct 2026).

### 7. Dracotail (Branded Dracotail)

- **Peak:** Aug 2025 to May 2026 (P7-P9).
- **Share:** 17.8% (P7), 23.9% (P8), 24.1% (P9). It was the most common premier top-cut deck in P8 and P9.
- **Key cards:** Dracotail Faimena, Dracotail Mululu, Branded in High Spirits, Albion the Branded Dragon.
- **Game plan:** Fusion Dragon deck run with the Branded engine. It fuses on both turns and recurs its Fusion monsters.
- **Notable results:** YCS São Paulo (11 Oct 2025), YCS Pittsburgh (8 Nov 2025, 1,847 players), YCS Guatemala City (11 Apr 2026), YCS Cartagena (2 May 2026), YCS Columbus (23 May 2026, 1,618 players).
- **Banlist:** Dracotail Mululu and Ketu Dracotail to Limited (2 Feb 2026). Rahu Dracotail to Limited, and Dracotail Lukias and Dracotail Arthalion to Semi-Limited (May 2026). Branded-engine hits (see Branded Despia) also applied.

### 8. Vanquish Soul (K9 Vanquish Soul)

- **Peak:** Jun 2025 to Feb 2026 (P6 by Tier-2+ data, P8-P9 by premier data).
- **Share:** 16.5% premier in P8. 10.1% of Tier-2+ top cuts in P6.
- **Key cards:** Vanquish Soul Razen, Vanquish Soul Dr. Mad Love, K9-17 Izuna, Stake your Soul!.
- **Game plan:** Interaction-heavy midrange. It reveals cards to trigger quick effects on either turn. The K9 package adds disruption and Xyz plays.
- **Notable results:** European WCQ (25 Jul 2025, 2,205 players), World Championship 2025 (Julien Kehon, 30-31 Aug 2025; 9 of the 27 Worlds decks were Vanquish Soul), YCS Bologna (22 Nov 2025, 2,429 players), NA Remote Duel YCS (6 Dec 2025), 300th YCS Dortmund (14 Feb 2026, 2,658 players), 300th YCS Guadalajara (14 Feb 2026).
- **Banlist:** Vanquish Soul Razen and Stake your Soul! to Limited (15 Sep 2025; both back to 3 on 2 Feb 2026). Vanquish Soul Hollie Sue, K9-66a Jokul and "A Case for K9" to Limited (2 Feb 2026). K9-04 Noroi Forbidden (May 2026).

### 9. Tenpai Dragon

- **Peak:** May 2024 to Dec 2024 (P3-P4).
- **Share:** 16.9% (P3), 16.1% (P4). Second-most common deck at the 2024 NAWCQ (about 400 of 2,635 players; 13 of the Top 64, Konami).
- **Key cards:** Tenpai Dragon Chundra, Tenpai Dragon Paidra, Sangen Summoning, Sangen Kaimen.
- **Game plan:** Aggressive FIRE Dragon deck that Synchro Summons during its own Battle Phase to win in one turn. It is very strong going second.
- **Notable results:** European WCQ (26 Jul 2024, 2,285 players), NA Remote Duel YCS (9 Nov 2024). Worlds 2024 Top 8.
- **Banlist:** Sangen Summoning to Limited (2 Sep 2024). Tenpai Dragon Chundra and Sangen Kaimen to Limited (Dec 2024).

### 10. Branded Despia (2024 "Branded Despia"; 2026 "Despia Branded")

- **Peak:** Spring to summer 2024 (minor), then Feb to Sep 2026 (main).
- **Share:** 15.4% in P10. Tier 2 in P9.
- **Key cards:** Aluber the Jester of Despia, Branded in High Spirits, Albion the Branded Dragon, Super Polymerization.
- **Game plan:** Fusion midrange. It fuses on both turns, often using the opponent's monsters with Super Polymerization.
- **Notable results:** UK Nationals (15 Jun 2024, 1,300 players), 13 National and 2 State Championships in 2026 (the largest was Spain, 410 players). European WCQ 2026 Top 4. Worlds 2026: runner-up Josh Kelly played a Light and Darkness Ritual/Branded hybrid, and one Top 8 deck was Branded Despia. It has no YCS or WCQ main-event win in the data.
- **Banlist:** Branded Fusion to Limited (2 Sep 2024), Semi-Limited (15 Sep 2025), and Limited again (May 2026). Branded in High Spirits to Semi-Limited (21 Sep 2026), then Limited (19 Oct 2026). Branded Opening to Limited and Albion the Branded Dragon Forbidden (19 Oct 2026). These Branded cards were also core to Branded Dracotail.

### 11. Yubel (Fiendsmith Yubel, Fiendsmith Kashtira Yubel)

- **Peak:** Jul 2024 to Dec 2024 (P3-P4).
- **Share:** 4.2% (P3), then 21.7% (P4), the highest of that period. At NAWCQ 2024 it was 266 of 2,635 players (about 10%) and 9 of the Top 64 (Konami).
- **Key cards:** Spirit of Yubel, Nightmare Throne, Samsara D Lotus, Fiendsmith Engraver.
- **Game plan:** Control. It builds a resilient Fiend board and burns or removes the opponent's monsters through Yubel's effects.
- **Notable results:** World Championship 2024 (Ruben Penaranda, 7-8 Sep 2024, Seattle; 6 of 28 Worlds decks were Yubel), YCS Lille (28 Sep 2024, 1,897 players), YCS Cancún (5 Oct 2024), TEAM YCS Santiago (28 Sep 2024).
- **Banlist:** Phantom of Yubel to Limited (Dec 2024). Fiendsmith's Lacrima Forbidden (2 Sep 2024) and Opening of the Spirit Gates Limited (2 Sep 2024) also hit it.

### 12. Light and Darkness Ritual ("Ritual of Light and Darkness", "LADR"; Azamina / Dark Magician / Mitsurugi variants)

- **Peak:** Jul 2026 to now (P10-P11).
- **Share:** 6.9% (P10), 29.7% (P11, only 64 entries). On Tier-2+ data it was the second-most common deck in P10 (12.1%) and the most common in P11 (23.9%).
- **Key cards:** Skull Archfiend of Chaos, Magician of Dark Chaos - Black Chaos, Ragged Records of Rites, Diabellstar the Black Witch.
- **Game plan:** Ritual combo that searches and Ritual Summons Chaos monsters, with Dark Magician or Azamina support for extra bodies and negation.
- **Notable results:** Oceanic WCQ (11 Jul 2026), World Championship 2026 runner-up (Josh Kelly; 11 of the 30 Worlds decks used it in some form), YCS Paris (19 Sep 2026, 2,588 players), YCS Houston (17 Oct 2026, 1,074 players). At NAWCQ 2026 it was among the most played decks but missed the Top 8 (ygoprodeck event breakdown).
- **Banlist (inferred):** On 19 Oct 2026, Magicians' Souls, Red-Eyes Dark Dragoon and Zenna's Deceiving Doll Maidens went Forbidden and Griffoh went Limited. All four are in the YCS Houston winning list, and Azamina Ilia Silvia (also Forbidden) is in the Azamina variant. Konami gives no reasons for TCG lists, so treat this as my inference from decklists.

### 13. Elfnote (often with Power Patron)

- **Peak:** Jun 2026 to now (P10-P12).
- **Share:** 6.3% (P10), 34.4% (P11, only 64 entries). Tier-2+: 10.6% (P10), 22.7% (P11), 18.2% (P12).
- **Key cards:** Elfnote Lucina, Elfnote Tinia, Elfnote Power Patron, Prohibited Power Patron Portal - Terminus.
- **Game plan:** Synchro combo that searches through Elfnote Power Patron and strips cards on the opponent's turn.
- **Notable results:** YCS Montreal (15 Aug 2026, 755 players), YCS Guayaquil (3 Oct 2026). One deck in the Worlds 2026 top 32.
- **Banlist:** Elfnote Tinia to Limited (21 Sep 2026), then Forbidden (19 Oct 2026). Terminus to Limited (21 Sep 2026); it is shared with Power Patron and DoomZ.

### 14. Voiceless Voice

- **Peak:** Feb 2024 to Aug 2024 (P2-P3).
- **Share:** 10.2% (P2, only 226 entries), 5.7% (P3).
- **Key cards:** Lo, the Prayers of the Voiceless Voice; Saffira, Dragon Queen of the Voiceless Voice; Skull Guardian, Protector of the Voiceless Voice.
- **Game plan:** LIGHT Ritual midrange. It sets up repeatable negation and protection, and its main deck is full of hand traps.
- **Notable results:** Many top cuts but few wins. Dominican Republic Nationals 2024 is its only premier win in the data. Konami named it among the decks with "similar numbers" behind the top three at NAWCQ 2024.
- **Banlist:** No Voiceless Voice card hit on a TCG list in the window.

### 15. Memento (Mementotlan, Goblin Biker Memento)

- **Peak:** Mar 2025 to Jul 2025 (P5-P6).
- **Share:** 2.6% (P5), 5.1% (P6).
- **Key cards:** Mementotlan Angwitch, Mementotlan Fusion, Mementomictlan Tecuhtlica - Creation King.
- **Game plan:** Fusion combo. It mills its own monsters and fuses into a large boss with protection.
- **Notable results:** YCS Houston (5 Apr 2025, 1,496 players), YCS Mexico City (19 Apr 2025, 1,432 players), Italy Nationals (7 Jun 2025, 1,418 players).
- **Banlist:** No Memento card hit on a TCG list in the window.

**How sharp is the line at 15?**

- **Sharp from 1 to 9.** Every deck there has at least one period at 15%+ of premier top cuts, several major wins, and multiple banlist hits.
- **Fairly sharp from 10 to 13.** Branded Despia, Yubel, Light and Darkness Ritual and Elfnote each reached tier 1 in at least one period and changed the banlist.
- **Soft at 14-15.** Voiceless Voice, Memento, Blue-Eyes, Labrynth, Sky Striker and Mermail all sit between about 0.9% and 1.9% TW. Voiceless Voice wins on share but has almost no wins. Memento has two YCS titles. Sky Striker has the biggest single win (a WCQ) but low share. Swapping any two of these would be defensible.

## 4. Ties and merges: the rules used

**General rule:** One entry per deck family. A family is defined by the archetype that supplies most of the main deck and the main win condition. Generic engines spliced into many decks (Fiendsmith, Bystial, Kashtira, Azamina, Radiant Typhoon, @Ignister) do not get their own entry. This mostly follows ygoprodeck's primary-archetype label.

| Case | Decision | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Snake-Eye vs Fire King Snake-Eye vs Diabellstar / Fiendsmith / Kashtira Snake-Eye | **One deck.** In 2024 the "Fire King" lists were Diabellstar Snake-Eye Fire King, with the same engine and the same banlist hits. | Split, Snake-Eye alone is 8.5% TW (still top 3) and Fire King alone is 5.2% TW (about #8). The split would push Memento out of the 15. Pure Fire King without Snake-Eye exists (YCS Birmingham 2025 winner; Jurrac Fire King 2026), but it is small. |
| Yubel vs Fiendsmith Yubel | **One deck.** Fiendsmith is an engine. | None. |
| Tenpai Dragon | **One deck.** | None. |
| Yummy vs Mitsurugi Yummy | **Merged into Yummy.** The Yummy cards are the core, and Yummy cards were hit. | If Mitsurugi Yummy went to Mitsurugi instead: Mitsurugi 9.9% TW (still #3, close to #2), Yummy 3.6% (about #9). |

Notes on the TCG side:

- Two newer cards Toon decks use are already TCG-legal. Rainbow Bridge Bifrost came out in the TCG on 3 Sep 2026, and Cartoon Shade in Beyond the Brave on 8 Oct 2026. Cartoon Shade is generic; it works with any monster that cannot be Normal Summoned. Neither made Toon a TCG deck.

### Key cards and engine

Names are from the ygoprodeck card database. Most cards are OCG-only, so these are unofficial translations until the TCG prints them. Blue-Eyes Ultimate Toon Dragon is the exception: Konami's TCG product page uses that name.

- **Toon World the Perfect World** (Field Spell; also called "Perfect Toon World"). Its name becomes "Toon World" on the field. It searches a "Toon" card, or a card that names one, up to three times per turn. It can also banish one of your Toon monsters until a card or effect resolves, which dodges removal.
- **Funny Dark Rabbit** (Level 4). It gives you an extra Normal Summon of a monster that mentions "Toon World". It also adds or places a Toon Field or Continuous Spell from the Deck, which is usually Perfect World.
- **Comic Cat** (Level 1). As a Main Phase Quick Effect, it Tributes a monster to Special Summon a "Toon World" monster from the hand or Deck, ignoring summoning conditions. With Toon World, it can Tribute the opponent's monster.
- **Evil Box** (Level 6). It Special Summons itself and gets a Toon Trap from the Deck. It also recycles a card from either Graveyard.
- **Dark-Eyes Illusionist Faceless Mage**. Discard it to place Mind Scan, or to revive a "Toon World" monster.
- **Mind Scan** (new Trap). It keeps the opponent's hand revealed, and once per turn it negates a declared card name.
- **Blue-Eyes Ultimate Toon Dragon** (Level 12 Fusion). It lets Toon monsters attack directly and recovers a Toon card from the Graveyard.
- **Older cards still core:** Toon Table of Contents, Toon Bookmark, Toon Terror. All five winning or top lists I read run 3 Perfect World, 3 Toon Table of Contents, 3 Toon Bookmark, 3 Funny Dark Rabbit and 1-3 Comic Cat. Several also run 3 Rainbow Bridge Bifrost as an extra way to search Perfect World.

**Game plan (one sentence):** A combo-control deck. It searches Toon World the Perfect World several ways, chains Funny Dark Rabbit's extra Normal Summon into Comic Cat and Evil Box to pull Toon monsters from the Deck, and ends on a board of Toon Terror, Mind Scan and Comic Cat's quick Tribute (which can remove an opponent's monster), with Perfect World's banish protecting the Toons.

### Where Toon would sit if OCG counted

- Toon was a top-two OCG deck for about four months (June to September 2026), with 20-28% of top cuts. It won the Japan Championship, a CCT and a China WCQ regional, and was the most common deck at YCSJ Osaka.
- On this report's time-weighted measure, four months at about 22% works out to about 2.4-3% over 36 months. That is close to Yubel (2.7%) and Branded Despia (3.5%). With its major wins and an OCG banlist hit, it would sit **around #10-12**, between Branded Despia and Light and Darkness Ritual.
