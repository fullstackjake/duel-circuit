# Deck shuffling (2026-11-07)

## The rule (Konami, Yu-Gi-Oh! TCG Rulebook v9, yugioh-card.com/eu/wp-content/uploads/2022/07/Rulebook_v9_en.pdf)

- Deck Zone: "If a card effect requires you to reveal cards from your Deck, or look through it, shuffle it and put it back
  in this space after resolving the effect."
- Glossary, Search: "You must shuffle your Deck after any time you search it and let your opponent shuffle or cut. You
  cannot activate an effect to search your Deck for a card if there are no cards that meet the requirements in your Deck."
- Card text overrides the rulebook: effects that return revealed cards in a set order keep that order.

## What the game did (diagnosis, harness-proven)

- A card leaving the Deck by an effect: the core shuffles at the end of that chain link (field.cpp shuffle_deck_check,
  processor.cpp ExecuteOperation). Correct.
- An effect that looks through the Deck and finds nothing (Sangan, Witch, floaters) or an optional search that is
  declined: no shuffle. **Wrong by the rulebook.**
- Effects that need a matching card to activate (Reinforcement of the Army) can't activate without one. Correct.
- No shuffle animation or sound; MSG_SHUFFLE_DECK was dropped before the board.

## The owner's decisions

1. Shuffle after any effect looks through the Deck, found or not: a core patch in patches/ygopro-core behind a new duel
   flag, on for new duels; old replays keep the old behaviour (replay version 6).
2. A shuffle animation (cut and riffle, about 0.45 s at the speed setting, a soft sound, skipped in fast-forward) on every
   deck shuffle for both players, shorter after the first, and once per deck at duel start.
3. M goes from the Battle Phase to Main Phase 2 (built).

## As built

### The rule in the core (decision 1)

`patches/ygopro-core/0003-shuffle-after-deck-look.patch` (applied by `tools/build_core.sh`, which copies the dylib to
`unity/Assets/Plugins/macOS`), behind `DUEL_SHUFFLE_AFTER_DECK_LOOK` = `DuelFlags.ShuffleAfterDeckLook`
(`0x4000000000000000`, src/Ygo.Engine/Constants.cs). The flag is part of `DuelFlags.ModeMR5`, the `DuelSettings` default,
so every new duel has it: the app, the harness and CPU-vs-CPU.

While an effect's **operation** resolves, the Deck is marked "looked at" when:

- `Duel.SelectMatchingCard` / `Duel.SelectTarget` searches a LOCATION_DECK, even when nothing matches (no prompt);
- a card prompt (SelectCard, SelectUnselectCard, SelectSum) shows Deck cards: `Group:Select`, `FilterSelect`,
  `aux.SelectUnselectGroup`;
- `Duel.ConfirmCards` reveals Deck cards;
- the chain link's operation info (`SetOperationInfo` / `SetPossibleOperationInfo`) names the player's LOCATION_DECK,
  with no cards named, for a search, Special Summon, send to the GY or banish. This covers the roughly 280 scripts that
  search through `GetMatchingGroup(..., LOCATION_DECK)` and then select, which show no prompt when nothing matches. Of
  about 3,100 scripts with such operation info, most also use SelectMatchingCard and were already covered.
  Not counted: to-Deck, mill (DECKDES, whose parameter is a count), discard or draw categories.

At the end of the operation, a looked-at Deck is shuffled once (`shuffle_deck_check`, the same shuffle the core does when
a card leaves the Deck). That happens unless the effect **kept the order**, in which case the card text wins. The effect
kept the order when, from its own script (not a filter, condition or value function it calls), it:

- used `ConfirmDecktop`, `GetDecktopGroup`, `GetDeckbottomGroup`, `SortDecktop` or `SortDeckbottom`;
- put a card on top of or under the Deck;
- moved Deck cards under `DisableShuffleCheck`. Only the Deck it moved cards in keeps its order.

Looks are also ignored under `DisableShuffleCheck`, and the script's own `ShuffleDeck` clears a pending look, so there is
never a double shuffle. Looks during costs and targets don't count; the rule says "after resolving the effect".

### Replays

A replay stores its flags, so replays recorded without the flag play back exactly as before, including the shipped Watch
the combo replays in `campaign/replays`. A replay with the flag is written as format 6 (src/Ygo.Engine/Replays/Replay.cs),
which has the same layout as 5, so a build before this change refuses it rather than playing it out of sync. Without the
flag it is still written as 4 (no mat) or 5. The app's `-replaycheck` checks both.

### Tests

`--shuffle-test` (src/Ygo.Harness/ShuffleTest.cs), 12 cases, each with the flag and, where it matters, without it:

- Sangan finding nothing shuffles;
- a declined search, a `Group:Select` and a look at the opponent's Deck each shuffle that Deck;
- a `GetMatchingGroup` search finding nothing shuffles (the operation-info detector; with no operation info, it doesn't);
- looks in the cost or target don't shuffle;
- a look plus `ShuffleDeck` shuffles once;
- a filter reading the top card doesn't cancel a look;
- a `DisableShuffleCheck` excavate and a card put on top keep their order;
- Reinforcement can't activate with no Warrior;
- a successful search produces the same messages with and without the flag.

These passed after the change: `--replay-test --games 12`, `--headliner-test`, `--analyze-test`, `--campaign-test`,
`--battle-misplay-test`, `--forced-attack-test`, `--sense-test`, `--removal-timing-test`, and the app's `-replaycheck`
and `-boardcheck`.

### Remaining gaps

- Effects whose operation info names "hand or Deck" (or "Deck or GY") count as a Deck look even when the player takes
  from the other place.
- A whole-Deck look made only through `GetMatchingGroup` plus a count or a random pick, with no prompt, reveal or
  matching operation info, isn't seen.
- A script that picks from its top cards without any of the order-keeping calls above, and expects the rest to keep
  their order, would now be shuffled. None was found, but the about 160 scripts that put Deck cards on top weren't read
  one by one.
- Continuous effects (no chain link) are covered by the select and reveal detectors, not by operation info.

### Baselines shift

Any duel that hits a Deck look that used to leave the Deck alone now draws differently from then on. Fixed-seed results
recorded before this change (QA runs, `-qaplay` seeds, deck ratings, studies, misplay hunts, calibration sets) no longer
reproduce exactly and should be re-run before they are compared. For example, the replay test's 12 bot games changed when
the operation-info detector was added (average code 903 to 890 characters). Duels are still deterministic for a given seed.
