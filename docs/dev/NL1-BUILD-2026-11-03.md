## Owner rules (bugs if broken)

- CPUs play their decks at full strength (skill 1.0 for builds). Difficulty is tuned by what goes into a build before it
  ships, never by weakening it; a build that misses its band stays `ready: no`.
- Every DP change goes through `Profile.Spend`/`Earn` with a ledger kind; nothing may be DP-positive.
- Old saves must load. No new save lines (spec §12).
- No real player's name anywhere in the game: no `#source` lines in decks/no-limits; provenance in SOURCES.md only.
- Commit nothing. Never touch `vendor/ygopro-core`, `build/play-alley`, `build/rework-economy`, `build/qa-night5`.

## Choices the spec left open (decided here)

1. **`-alleycheck` step numbers.** The show layer already owns steps 16-18 (and 16b). NL-1's four steps are **19-22**
   (spec §14.3 "16-19" → 19 locked, 20 panel, 21 versus, 22 forced win). `-alleycheck` 1-22 must pass.
2. **Build blocks and deck files must agree.** C1a writes each `build:` block from spec §5.3 as written. C1b builds the
   decks from §5.3. Where C1b has to deviate (a pick that can't be played, Imogen's Chaos Sorcerer swap), it records the
   final `restored:`/`over:`/`pieces:`/`stakes:` per build in its report and the coordinator reconciles alley-events.txt;
   `AlleyData.Problems` is the check.
3. **`ready:` at ship.** Every wave-1 build and the table start `ready: no`. C3's `--alley-study --only "No Limits"` and
   `--endless-test` decide `ready: yes` (the ship gate, spec §14.3); the coordinator flips them.
4. **The difficulty pass's levers** (owner decisions already made): Dumpster Row by its `closer:`, bases and alley.txt
   curated list (never a weaker CPU); Nyx's seats 1-3 with era-true, older decks (META-DECKS decision 5: not meta
   builds); the Brawl by the out-of-towners after The Black Market (spec §8), a fourth Basement card as the next lever,
   payouts unchanged.

## Reporting

Each agent ends with: files changed; checks run with their exact counts (what passed, what failed, what wasn't run);
every deviation from the spec and why; open questions for the owner. Short sentences. Audio is never heard.
