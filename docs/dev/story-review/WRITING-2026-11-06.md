# Story review: the player-facing writing (Narrative Designer, 2026-11-06)

Read-only. I read campaign/story.txt (197 scenes), alley-voice.txt, van.txt, shop.txt, the profiles of every speaker
who appears in a scene or pool, and the story docs. I left the `place:` keys alone. Line numbers are story.txt's unless
a file is named. I left out what docs/dev/STORY-REVIEW-2026-11-01.md already fixed, and only mention its open items where
they're still unfixed.

**Verdict.** The voices are in good shape. The No Limits pilots are the best-differentiated group in the game: each has
a job vocabulary (DJ, nurse, IT support, cartoonist, doorman) and a wound, and none of them could swap lines with another.
Moth, Baz, Nocturne and Penny also hold up well, and the held-card payoff (`held-card-home`) is the best scene since
Gus's corkboard. The main problem is mechanical. Scenes keep repeating the line the player saw a second earlier: the
results screen's lose line comes back in the gift and beat scenes, and the versus intro comes back in the entrance
scenes. This happens in about 45 scenes, and fixing it is text-only. The second problem is continuity that drifted when
the data changed but the words didn't: Nyx's seat 2, the wave-2 speakers, and who holds the end chair.

## 1. Repetition the player sees back to back (systemic)

**1.1 Gift scenes repeat the results screen's lose line.** The 2026-11-01 review fixed this for the cup clears but not
for the `beat:` gifts. The results screen shows the profile's `lose:`, then the gift scene opens with the same words.
- Verbatim or near-verbatim (15 of 21): frog-ftk 90, six-samurai 96, geargia 102, blackwing 107, lightsworn 113,
  infernity 119, goat-control 125, true-draco 131, plant-synchro 137, shaddoll 143, drytron 149, kozmo 154,
  tearlaments 159, snake-eye 166, mitsurugi 172.
- **Fix:** rewrite each first line so it reacts to the handover rather than the loss. Examples:
  - geargia 102: "Shop rule? Fine. Engineers pay their debts in parts." (instead of "Somebody threw a wrench...")
  - lightsworn 113: "Even the dawn pays up, eventually. Hold out your hand."
  - true-draco 131: "Tribute, then. A king who won't pay it isn't one."
  - shaddoll 143: "Don't look so pleased. I'm only giving you the one that kept trying to leave."
  - kozmo 154: "Pilots' rules, then. You shot it down, you keep it." (drop "My fleet's grounded. Nice flying.")
  - snake-eye 166: "Here. Something to keep you warm." (drop "My flames went out. Good duel.")
  - infernity 119: "...Fine. I'll give it away instead. That's almost like keeping nothing." (drop the first sentence)

**1.2 Headliner, pilot and out-of-towner entrance scenes repeat the versus intro.** The entrance scene plays just before
the duel, then the versus screen draws an intro from a pool that holds the same line.

  (alley-voice.txt:338-532).

  nl-oot-augie 1200 ("One hound short tonight") and nl-oot-sasha 1195 each echo their `outoftowner:intro`.
