## Who kept the key before Nyx

**Penny.** (Owner decision 11, 2026-11-06: unanswered in the game, answered here.)

- Penny drove the number nine out of the bus depot for thirty years and brought the last bus in the night the depot
  closed (2014, the year before her first Friday out back).
- The brick-red steel door in the alley wall is the depot's old yard door: the drivers' way out to the bins and the
  back of the shop's street. The council padlocked the front gates. Nobody asked for the yard-door key, and Penny kept
  it on her transit lanyard.
- When Nyx set up her table under the lantern, against that wall, Penny gave her the key. Nyx keeps things that
  should rest; Penny thought the depot should.
- Nyx won't say because Penny asked her not to ("The card won't like the fuss"), and because Nyx doesn't tell other
  people's stories. Nyx lets the out-of-towners in; that was Nyx's choice, not Penny's, and Penny doesn't mind.
- Nocturne doesn't know, and has asked (`nl-clear`). Gus remembers Penny drove the nine (`nl-invitational-home`); he
  doesn't know about the key.

**Rules for later lines:**
- Penny never mentions the key. Her only tells are the ones shipped: she knew the depot ("I drove the number nine out
  of there for thirty years") and isn't surprised the door is open ("Lovely echo. The card won't like it.").
- Nyx never names the key's last keeper, and nobody else learns it.
- Nobody says the door wasn't there before (No Limits continuity 1).

## Settled in the 2026-11-06 writing pass

- **Baz's name for Gus is UPSTAIRS** (`finale`). Baz gave it to you first, on your first night ("Then you're
  Upstairs"), and only ever lent it out. Before the finale, Baz never names him (`alley-baz`: "He doesn't get a name.
  He knows why.").
- **Kit's lost card:** in Kit's first year out back, Kit staked a card at Orvel's Table that wasn't really a spare (it
  was in no deck, so the House's rule let it through). Kit lost, and it went off Moth's rail before Kit claimed it. Kit never names it. It's why Kit antes spares only,
  and why Kit won't go through the depot door (`nl-wall-kit`; `first-railgone` is the echo).
- **The Duchess and the stamp:** Ilse asked Hollis for a Format Master stamp once and wouldn't play a year's own cards
  to earn it (`first-stamp`). It's the setup for "I never once beat a year with its own cards" (`every-era-clear`).
  Gus's corkboard has held her gold-ink card at the top since before Vera could shuffle (`graveyard-kings-clear`).
- **Gus oiled the back door** after the held card went home (`held-card-home`); the footer echoes it. Lines before
  that point say it sticks; lines after say it doesn't. The `finale` says neither.
- **Junia's word for you after the Invitational** is Baz's chalk name ({nick}); "Local" is retired then
  (`nl-invitational-win`). Her summer turns to September there.
- **Ribbit Ruiz is "the Bubblegum Loop"**; "Mass Driver" belongs to Mass Driver Moss alone.
