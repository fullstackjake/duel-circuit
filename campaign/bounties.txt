# Gus's bounty board (CARD-UNLOCKS-SPEC-2026-11-01): three bounties per band, each paid once per save.
#   band: <band>[, <circuit band>...]   the board; circuits of the bands after the first count on it too
#   bounty: <id> | <checks> | <text>    every check must hold in a one-on-one circuit duel you win in that band
# Checks:
#   finisher:normal   a Normal Monster's attack took their last LP
#   finisher:extra    an Extra Deck monster's attack (Fusion, Synchro, Xyz, Link) took it
#   finisher:effect   effect damage (not battle) took it
#   nodamage          you took no damage (LP paid as a cost doesn't count)
#   byturn:N          won by turn N (turns count both players')
#   comeback:LP       your LP fell to LP or less
#   bighit:N          one hit of N damage or more
#   turn:N            N damage or more in one turn
#   summons:N         N summons or more in one of your turns
#   traps:N           N trap activations or more
#   second            you went second
#   own               your own deck (no loaner)
# A bounty pays the card your Recipe misses most (in the shop's era, not on the no-pass list); with no Recipe set,
# the band's first pays 1 Card Token and the rest are owed until you set one.

band: Beginner
bounty: beginner-vanilla | finisher:normal | Finish a duel with a Normal Monster's attack
bounty: beginner-clean | nodamage | Win without taking a point of damage
bounty: beginner-quick | byturn:7 | Win by turn 7

band: Novice
bounty: novice-comeback | comeback:1000 | Win after falling to 1000 LP or less
bounty: novice-bighit | bighit:3000 | Deal 3000 damage or more in one hit
bounty: novice-traps | own traps:2 | Activate 2 traps and win with your own deck

band: Intermediate
bounty: intermediate-extra | finisher:extra | Finish a duel with an Extra Deck monster's attack
bounty: intermediate-swarm | summons:5 | Summon 5 times in one turn
bounty: intermediate-second | second byturn:9 | Go second and win by turn 9

band: Advanced
bounty: advanced-traps | traps:4 | Activate 4 traps in one duel
bounty: advanced-otk | turn:8000 | Deal 8000 damage in one turn
bounty: advanced-burn | finisher:effect | Finish a duel with effect damage

band: Expert
bounty: expert-comeback | second comeback:2000 | Go second, fall to 2000 LP or less, and win
bounty: expert-clean | own nodamage | Win with your own deck without taking damage
bounty: expert-swarm | summons:8 | Summon 8 times in one turn

band: Master, Gauntlet
bounty: master-quick | byturn:4 | Win by turn 4
bounty: master-otk | second turn:8000 | Go second and deal 8000 damage in one turn
bounty: master-vanilla | own finisher:normal | Finish a duel with a Normal Monster's attack, with your own deck
