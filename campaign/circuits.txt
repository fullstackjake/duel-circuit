# Duel Circuit campaign: circuits in order. Clear every match in a circuit to open the next one.
#   circuit: <name>            starts a circuit
#   band: <difficulty>         the difficulty band it belongs to (a heading in the list)
#   era: <year>                card shop sets released up to this year open with the circuit
#   banlist: <list name>       the era's Forbidden/Limited list (lflists/history.lflist.conf): in era-limited play your
#                              team's decks must be legal under it; otherwise nobody answers to it, and the CPUs never
#                              do. The Beginner circuits play house rules (no list)
#   blurb: <one line>          shown under its name
#   prize: <DP>                paid the first time you clear it
#   match: <character> <deck>  an opponent (personalities/<character>.profile) with one of the decks in decks/
# Bands follow measured deck strength (campaign/deck-ratings.txt: bot vs bot Elo), not release year; each circuit
# groups decks from nearby years. The opponents' skill also rises band by band. The late bands (Expert, Master,
# Gauntlet) pay more per win and bigger prizes, plus Card Tokens per clear: by then a player needs several top decks.
# The Gauntlet brings back four of the strongest decks (not the Houston bosses just beaten) so the finale is hardest.
