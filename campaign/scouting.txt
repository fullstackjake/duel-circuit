deck: shaddoll
plan: Shaddoll Fusion makes El Shaddoll Construct or Winda, even from the Deck when you have an Extra Deck monster out. Every Shaddoll sent to the GY by an effect triggers: searches, draws, pops, revivals.
keys: Shaddoll Fusion; El Shaddoll Construct; El Shaddoll Winda; Shaddoll Beast
beats it: Banishing turns off their GY triggers: D.D. Crow, Macro Cosmos or Dimensional Fissure. Winda lets each player Special Summon only once per turn, so remove it before your big turn.
build: D.D. Crow; Macro Cosmos; Dimensional Fissure; Dark Ruler No More; Called by the Grave

deck: danger-dark-world
plan: Each Danger! monster reveals itself to make them discard at random; it pays off whatever goes, summoning, searching, destroying and drawing. Card Destruction and Allure of Darkness keep the hand full.
keys: Danger! Nessie!; Danger! Bigfoot!; Danger!? Jackalope?; Card Destruction
beats it: Hand traps early: Ash Blossom on Nessie's search or a draw spell. Nibiru, the Primal Being once they've summoned five times. Clear their bodies before they become Knightmare Links.
build: Ash Blossom & Joyous Spring; Nibiru, the Primal Being; Droll & Lock Bird; Effect Veiler

deck: drytron

build: Ash Blossom & Joyous Spring; Effect Veiler; Infinite Impermanence; Nibiru, the Primal Being; Ghost Ogre & Snow Rabbit

deck: tenpai-dragon

keys: Sangen Summoning; Tenpai Dragon Chundra; Tenpai Dragon Genroku; Sangen Kaimen

build: Gameciel, the Sea Turtle Kaiju; Swords of Revealing Light; Ash Blossom & Joyous Spring; Effect Veiler; Infinite Impermanence
turn: second - Tenpai wants to go second and clear your board; make it go first instead

deck: mitsurugi

turn: second - with the strongest decks against it, going second won 55% and going first 41% (the sixth card and the first attack beat its turn-1 board)
